The only constraints are the color palette, font, and the scroll-driven 3D narrative direction described below.

## 🎬 The Core Idea: Scroll-Driven 3D Cinematic Storytelling

The entire website is one continuous **scroll-driven 3D journey**. The camera moves through a living 3D world. Sections don't "appear" — they are **revealed** as the user travels through the scene.

Use **`@react-three/fiber`**, **`@react-three/drei`**, **GSAP ScrollTrigger**, and **Lenis smooth scroll** to orchestrate this.

### The Narrative Arc (AI decides exact choreography, timing, easing, and staging):

**Act 1 — Hero**
A laptop sits on the left side of the scene, slightly angled, screen glowing. The user's photo is displayed on the laptop screen. Hero text floats on the right. The scene breathes — subtle ambient float, mouse parallax. Everything feels alive.

**Act 2 — The Pull**
As the user begins scrolling, the camera starts a slow cinematic pull toward the laptop. The laptop gradually moves to the center of the viewport. The hero text fades and dissolves. The camera continues drifting — closer, closer — until it passes *through* the laptop screen. A portal moment.

**Act 3 — About**
The camera emerges on the other side of the screen into a new environment — the About section world. This could be a desk scene, a void with floating code, a dark room — AI decides. Ravi's bio and details appear here, anchored in this new 3D space.

**Act 4 — Tech / Tools World**
As scrolling continues, the camera drifts through a floating graveyard of developer artifacts — a mechanical keyboard, a mouse, circuit fragments, `{ }` glyphs, terminal cursors, floating code lines. Each object drifts past the camera like debris in space. Tech stack information is revealed as these objects float by.

**Act 5 — Projects**
The camera arrives at a "gallery wall" or architectural space — project cards exist as physical objects in the 3D world. Hovering tilts them. Clicking navigates to individual project detail pages.

**Act 6 — Contact / Landing Zone**
The camera slows, settles. The environment calms. Contact form appears. Clean. Minimal. End of journey.

> **This is a directional brief, not a script.** The AI should treat this as inspiration and execute its own cinematic vision. The camera path, easing curves, object placement, scene composition, lighting moods, transition effects — all of it is the AI's creative call. If a better storytelling idea exists, use it.

## 🌐 3D Scene Details

**Objects to include (AI decides placement, scale, animation behavior):**
- Laptop (hero anchor — Ravi's photo on screen)
- Mechanical keyboard
- Computer mouse
- EV bike / car silhouette (nods to Yugo Rides)
- Code glyphs: `{ }`, `< />`, `//`, `> _`
- Circuit board fragments
- Floating holographic code lines / terminal text
- Particle field (white dots, subtle drift)

**All objects:** Grayscale, wireframe, or low-poly monochrome. No color tinting.

**Sources:** GLTF/GLB models from Sketchfab (free/CC), market.pmnd.rs, drei's built-in primitives, or procedurally generated. Credit sources in code comments.

**3D Behavior rules:**
- Mouse parallax on all floating objects
- Scroll = camera movement (not section jumping)
- Depth layers: foreground moves faster than background
- Lighting: dramatic — single key light, deep shadows, red rim light as accent
- No flat scenes — everything has depth, atmosphere, fog

## 📬 Contact & Backend

**Contact Form (on-page):**
- Fields: Name, Email, Message
- Submits to local Express backend
- Success toast on submit

**Backend (`/server` folder):**
- Node.js + Express.js + `better-sqlite3`
- Routes: `POST /api/contact`, `POST /api/leads`, `GET /api/health`
- CORS enabled

**Exit-Intent Popup:**
- Triggers on cursor approaching browser top
- Headline: *"Wait — let's stay connected"* (or AI picks something better)
- Fields: Name, Email, Profession — all optional
- Posts to `POST /api/leads`
- ESC key + X closes it
- Styled like a terminal card on dark overlay
